# Lumement privacy policy

*Last updated: [date of publishing]*

Lumement is a notification LED for Android phones, made by [developer's legal name]. This
policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **Lumement doesn't collect
anything. It has no internet access, so nothing it handles can leave your phone.**

## No internet, no accounts, no tracking

Lumement doesn't ask Android for internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere. It has no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking code.

## What Lumement reads on your phone, and why

**Notifications.** You give Lumement notification access so that it notices new messages. For
each notification, it looks at which app posted it, what kind of notification it is (for
example, a message or an ongoing download), its importance, and when the message was sent. It
uses these to decide whether to light the screen. **Lumement never reads, keeps or sends what
your messages say.** If you choose the "New message" screen, Lumement asks Android to show that
message again as a pop-up on your phone for up to 10 seconds, with your lock-screen privacy
settings applied. The message isn't stored.

**App icons and names.** To glow in each app's colour, Lumement reads the icon of the app that
sent the message and works out its main colour. It also reads the app's name for its Apps list.
That list can also show the other apps in your phone's app drawer, so you can set one up before
it ever sends a message. Lumement sees only apps that appear in the app drawer.

**Lumement effects (optional accessibility service).** If you turn it on, Lumement uses
Android's accessibility service to draw its effect and LED over the lock screen, and to turn the
screen off when no message is left to show. It's set up to receive no accessibility events and
to have no access to what's on your screen, so it can't read anything.

## What Lumement keeps on your phone

These stay in Lumement's private storage, which other apps can't read:
- your settings (the look, the LED's place and timing);
- the apps that have messaged you: their names, when each was first and last heard from, its
  colour, and your choices for it (muted, or your own colour);
- the messages still waiting, as Android's notification reference, the app and the time, so the
  LED comes back after a restart. Each one is removed once it's read;
- whether you own premium, and when its free trial started.

None of this is included in Android backups or moved to a new phone. Uninstalling Lumement, or
Settings → Apps → Lumement → Storage → Clear data, deletes all of it.

## Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Lumement never sees your payment
details or your Google account. It only asks Google Play, through the Play Store app on your
phone, whether premium is owned. Play's in-app review card, if you see it, is also shown by the
Play Store app.

## Children

Lumement isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. A change
that let Lumement collect anything would also need the app to ask for internet access, and the
Data safety section of its Play listing would change with it.
